## What Veesion does

- Gesture-recognition AI over existing in-store CCTV; detects concealment and other movement
  patterns that precede theft. Explicitly no facial recognition and no biometric identification.
- Two deployment shapes: a compact on-site server (NUC-class appliance) installed in the store,
  or a serverless integration, chosen by the store's internet setup.
- Output is a short video alert delivered to the store team's mobile application for human review.
- Sectors: supermarkets, hypermarkets, pharmacies, independent retail stores.
- Veesion SAS, RCS Nanterre 838 664 274, registered office 42 rue de Tauzia, 33800 Bordeaux,
  France. Managing Director: Benoit Koenig. Founded 2018; commercial launch 2021.
